What do SMA, RSI, returns, and volatility mean?

SMA is the average closing price over 20, 50, or 200 trading days and helps describe the trend.

Returns show the percentage price change over a selected period.

RSI measures recent momentum from 0 to 100; it does not independently prove a buy or sell.

Volatility describes how sharply price moves; higher values indicate larger historical swings.
